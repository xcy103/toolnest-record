## Today's Work

### 1. Integer-Cent Arithmetic

Added `lib/tip.ts` to parse bill amounts and percentages from decimal strings. Amounts use integer
cents and percentages use hundredths of a percent, rather than multiplying floating-point money
directly. The total tip is rounded once to the nearest cent, with half cents rounded up. Inputs
with too many decimal places, negative values, exponent notation or out-of-range values are rejected.

The maximum bill is 999,999,999.99 and the maximum rate is 100%. Those bounds keep the intermediate
integer product within JavaScript's safe-integer range. Zero bills and zero tips remain valid.

### 2. Exact Bill Splitting

Splitting rounds down to a base share and distributes the remaining cents to a second group.
For example, a 100.00 total split three ways produces two payments of 33.33 and one of 33.34.
All displayed payments add up to the total, with no more than one cent between individual shares.

### 3. Page and Bilingual Integration

amount formatting, explicit error states and a copyable breakdown. Currency selection changes
the display denomination only; there is no exchange-rate conversion or payment service.

The tool is registered in the Calculators category and directory, with English/Chinese messages,
localized titles, canonical and hreflang links, JSON-LD and sitemap entries. No new dependency,

## Learning Notes

- Money rounding is a product rule: round the bill-level tip once, then allocate whole cents.
- A split total needs a remainder policy, not just a rounded average multiplied by the headcount.
- Invariant tests complement example cases by checking that all payments sum to the total.
- Mobile QA should include shared navigation, not only the new page's own controls.
