## Learning Notes

- Durations are quantities, not wall-clock times. Integer seconds avoid date objects, time zones
  and unwanted 24-hour wrapping.
- Formatting the absolute magnitude and adding the sign afterward keeps negative borrowing
  results consistent across hours, minutes and seconds.
- Exact total seconds and rounded decimal conversions should be clearly distinguished.
