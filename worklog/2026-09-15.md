## Completed Work

### 1. Forward and Backward Working-Date Calculation

Added `/business-date-calculator` to move forward or backward by 0–9,999 working days. The starting
date is not counted: with a Saturday/Sunday weekend, Friday plus one working day becomes Monday, and
Monday minus one working day becomes Friday.

The result shows a localized full date, an ISO date, the requested direction and amount, total
calendar days moved and weekend days skipped. Calculations use UTC calendar fields so daylight-saving
changes cannot shift a date-only result.

### 2. Shared Weekend Rules and Validation

Reused the same configurable weekend selector and business-day module as the range calculator.
Custom schedules therefore behave consistently across both tools. Zero working days returns the
starting date, invalid amounts are rejected, and calculations that leave years 0001–9999 report a
localized range error.

Seven focused tests cover standard and custom weekends, include-end behaviour, forward and backward
movement, zero, invalid settings and date overflow. The earlier Date Calculator now also reuses a
shared local-Today formatter introduced during this work.

## Learning Notes

- A business-day offset must define whether the starting date counts. Excluding it matches the common
  meaning of “one working day after Friday” and keeps forward and backward calculations symmetrical.
- Sharing the weekend model between range counting and date movement prevents the two related tools
  from disagreeing on non-standard work weeks.
