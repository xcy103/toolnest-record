## Learning Notes

- Euclid's remainder algorithm reduces dimensions without changing their width:height order.
- Integer quotient/remainder comparisons make halves-up rounding explicit and avoid relying
  on decimal display precision.
- A truncated decimal display is approximate; showing the exact fraction retains the underlying
  calculation for repeating decimals.
