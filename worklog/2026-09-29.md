## Learning Notes

- Campaign links are safest to rebuild through `URL` and `URLSearchParams`, which apply standard
  percent/form encoding and avoid ad hoc query-string concatenation.
- Query parameters are ordered pairs rather than a plain object: repeated unrelated keys must not
  collapse while campaign parameters are replaced.
- Deleting every managed UTM key before appending current values prevents old optional values from
  silently leaking into a new campaign.
