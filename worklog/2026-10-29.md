## Learning Notes

- A datetime-local control needs both a fractional formatted value and an appropriate step
  to retain millisecond precision.
- Local calendar fields must be formatted consistently, including early positive years.
- These controls do not include UTC offsets. Existing daylight-saving ambiguity behavior
  is unchanged; the fix does not promise lossless conversion through every ambiguous local time.
