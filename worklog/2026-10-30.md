## Learning Notes

- Every complete seven-day block contains the same number of working and weekend
  days, regardless of its starting weekday or direction of travel.
- Reserving the final working day prevents a bulk jump from moving past the desired
  destination onto a trailing weekend.

  differences across arbitrary weekend configurations.
