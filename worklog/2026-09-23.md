## Completed Work

Added `/slug-generator` to turn a title or phrase into a readable URL path segment. Users can
choose a hyphen or underscore separator, lowercase the result, and choose whether to collapse
consecutive separators. The page provides copyable readable and URL-encoded outputs.

The generator decomposes common accented Latin letters and removes their accent marks, so `Café`
becomes `cafe` with lowercase enabled. Chinese characters remain in the readable result; the
encoded output uses standard percent encoding for direct use as a URL path segment. Punctuation
becomes a separator, and leading or trailing separators are removed. English and Chinese UI,
metadata, structured data, Text-category registration, search and sitemap coverage were added.

## Learning Notes

Readable slugs and URL-encoded path segments solve different parts of the same problem. Preserving
Chinese in the readable version keeps the text meaningful, while percent encoding gives a portable
ASCII representation for URLs. Making the duplicate-separator rule optional also makes the result
predictable when punctuation appears in the source title.
