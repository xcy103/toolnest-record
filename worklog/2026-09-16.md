## Completed Work

Added `/week-number` with ISO week number, ISO week-year and the Monday-to-Sunday date range for any
selected date. The calculation anchors each week on Thursday and defines week 1 as the week
containing January 4. This handles calendar-year boundaries: January 1, 2021 belongs to ISO week
53 of 2020, while December 31, 2024 belongs to ISO week 1 of 2025.

The tool uses UTC date-only arithmetic, runs in the browser, and includes English and Chinese copy,
copyable ISO week identifier, localized metadata and structured data. Registration adds it to the
Time category, search, the all-tools directory and the bilingual sitemap.

## Learning Notes

An ISO week-year is not always the same as the date's calendar year. Showing both the week-year and
the exact Monday/Sunday range makes that distinction visible instead of returning an ambiguous week
number alone.
