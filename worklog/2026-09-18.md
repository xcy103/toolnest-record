## Completed Work

Added `/age-calculator` with birth-date and as-of-date inputs, including a Today shortcut. The
result shows calendar age in years, months and days, total days lived, and the next birthday with
days remaining. Birth dates after the as-of date are rejected. A February 29 birthday is treated
as February 28 in non-leap years, and the page explains that rule and its general-use scope.

The calculation uses UTC date-only arithmetic to avoid time-zone and daylight-saving shifts.
English and Chinese UI, metadata, JSON-LD, Time-category registration, search and sitemap coverage
were added. Result and next-birthday values have copy controls.

## Learning Notes

Calendar age is not simply total days divided by 365: month lengths and leap years affect the
years/months/days breakdown. Making the February 29 convention explicit keeps the next-birthday
result predictable without presenting it as a legal or medical rule.
