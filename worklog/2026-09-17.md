## Completed Work

Added `/weekday` to show the weekday of a selected date and the next occurrence of a chosen weekday.
The search begins after the selected date. When the selected date already matches, the next result
is exactly seven days later. Both the weekday and the next date can be copied.

The tool uses the shared UTC calendar model, validates date and year limits, and has English and
Chinese UI, metadata and JSON-LD. Its Time-category registration includes search, category pages,
the tool directory and sitemap without additional routing code.

Browser QA verified February 29, 2024 is Thursday and that the next Thursday is March 7, 2024.
The result, selector and copy controls fit at 375px viewport width without overlap or horizontal
overflow. The same shared calendar test module covers leap day, same-day matching, year overflow and
ISO week boundaries for both new tools.

## Learning Notes

"Next Thursday" can mean today or a future Thursday unless the interface defines it. This tool
explicitly searches from tomorrow, making same-day results predictable and symmetric for every
weekday.
