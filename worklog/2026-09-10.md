## Learning Notes

- Terms and Privacy serve different purposes: Privacy describes data handling, while Terms define
  conditions for using the service and allocating responsibility.
- Legal copy should track what the product actually does and avoid unsupported company facts or
  jurisdiction-specific promises.
- A public repository is not automatically open source; an explicit license determines reuse rights.
- Policy pages need the same metadata, localization, navigation and responsive checks as tool pages.
